F1 in Schools is an international competition where student teams design, manufacture and race a miniature Formula 1 car — and have to run the team like a small company, with a brand, sponsors and communication.

It was the first thing I did outside of class.

## What I did

I was the team's **social media lead**. I developed and ran the digital communication strategy with three goals: give the team visibility, engage with the public and promote our sponsors. In practice that meant editing the videos, creating the visual and written content and looking after the brand.

The car's engineering was in my teammates' hands; my side was communication. I learned content creation, digital marketing and brand management — all as a team and on strict deadlines.
