A LENG1 project in the first semester of my first year. The brief: cool a small box without a compressor, using only a **Peltier cell** — a plate that, with current flowing, heats up on one side and cools down on the other.

## What I did

Once again, a bit of everything: assembling the insulated box, the electronics, the temperature sensors and the control code.

Heat from the hot side leaves through a CPU heatsink and fan mounted on the lid; inside, a small fan spreads the cold through the box.

![The aluminium heatsink and fan, mounted over the Peltier cell on the box lid.](./heatsink.jpg)

![The electronics inside the box: control board, sensors and wiring fixed to the foam walls.](./electronics.jpg)

## Result

With the room at about 20 °C, the inside reached about **7.9 °C** — the lowest we got.
