LSIS1 (Systems Laboratories 1) is the integrative course of the second year of Systems Engineering at ISEP. In 2026 the brief came from outside: **António Meireles S.A.**, a Portuguese household appliance manufacturer, wanted a digital after-sales portal to bring the company closer to its professional customers. Several teams competed, judged by a panel of lecturers and company representatives.

## What the portal does

- Warranty management.
- Technical support requests.
- Spare parts ordering.
- Tracking of after-sales processes.
- Management dashboards.

## What I did

There were four of us and I did a bit of everything — from the database to the interface, through the portal's logic. It was the first project with a real client: shifting requirements, tight deadlines and having to explain technical decisions to people who aren't engineers.

The code belongs to the team and the course, so it isn't public. ISEP's news piece about the challenge is linked above (in Portuguese).
