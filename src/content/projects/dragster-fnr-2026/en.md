Portuguese National Robotics Championship, Dragster category, with team **Tropa do Massas** (yes, that really is the name). The competition took place at the 26th National Robotics Festival — RoboCup Portugal Open, in Barcelos.

## What I did

- Built the robot from scratch: wiring schematics, custom PCBs for control and power distribution, and the full assembly.
- Wrote the C code integrating the sensors with a Teensy 4.0 microcontroller to control and correct the trajectory in real time.
- Handled communication with our sponsors: Gislotica, Critical TechWorks and PTRobotics.

## The competition

Three days of racing. Things broke, we did emergency repairs between rounds, and we made it to the finals: **8th of 16 teams** and the **Cheetah Award** (Prémio Chita).

## Inside

The control PCB, which I designed: the Teensy 4.0 in the middle, two Pololu TB9051FTG motor drivers, the power capacitors and the sensor connectors.
